Unofficial Galaga core for CollectorVision Phoenix
Should be compatible with both releases of hardware.

You will need to provide Galga ROMS - GALAGA, GALAGAO or GALAGAMW sets should work.

After the initial self-test, press reset to clear the information screen and start the game.

5 or SELECT for coin up. 1 or START for player 1 start, 2 for player 2 start.

The arcade is vertical. 0 or SNES shoulder button to rotate screen.
