SD card setup: put the BIOS files in an INTV folder (the root also works). The core picks each one out by size and
CRC, so the names don't matter. Your exec.bin, grom.bin, ecs.bin and sp0256-012.bin can go in as they are, and the
long voice-ROM name still works.

+----------------------------------------------------------+
¦            ROM            ¦ Size ¦  CRC-32  ¦ Needed for ¦
+---------------------------+------+----------+------------¦
¦ EXEC                      ¦ 8K   ¦ CBCE86F7 ¦ everything ¦
+---------------------------+------+----------+------------¦
¦ GROM                      ¦ 2K   ¦ 683A4158 ¦ everything ¦
+---------------------------+------+----------+------------¦
¦ ECS                       ¦ 24K  ¦ EA790A06 ¦ ECS games  ¦
+---------------------------+------+----------+------------¦
¦ Intellivoice (SP0256-012) ¦ 2K   ¦ 0DE7579D ¦ speech     ¦
+----------------------------------------------------------+

- Other versions: a different EXEC or GROM version is still used if it's named EXEC.BIN or GROM.BIN.
- Missing files:
- No EXEC or GROM: starting a game shows "NO EXEC/GROM IN /INTV".
- No ECS ROM: the ECS stays off.
- No voice ROM: the Intellivoice is switched off.
