Unofficial Time Pilot '84 core for CollectorVision Phoenix
Should be compatible with both releases of hardware.

You will need to provide Time Pilot 84 ROMS - tp84 or tp84a sets should work. tp84b will NOT work as it has a different memory map.

5 or SELECT for coin up. 1 or START for player 1 start, 2 for player 2 start.
Two fire buttons are fire and missile.

The arcade is vertical. 0 or SNES shoulder button to rotate screen.
